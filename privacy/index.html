Privacy

This policy explains how Skin Nourishers collects and uses your personal information, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect. When you send an enquiry we collect your name, and whichever of email or phone you give us, along with the treatment you asked about and anything you write in the message field.

Why. Solely to respond to your enquiry and arrange a consultation. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

Consent. Nothing is stored unless you tick the consent box. That box is never pre-ticked.

Your rights. You may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email info@skinnourishers.co.za and we will action it.

Photography. Treatment photographs are only ever published with the client’s written consent, and identifying features are obscured or cropped out.
